Stereo Jack:
1 Sleeve/GND
2 Ring
5 TIP

Note:
When unplugged the stereo jack pins 4 + 5 short to one another. Not needed for this project, just information.

RP2040 pin 14 to jack pin 5 (tip) ~1" magnet wire
RP2040 pin 15 to jack pin 2 (ring) ~3/4" magnet wire
RP2040 pin GND to jack pin 1 (sleeve) ~3/4" magnet wire

Use fingernail file to remove enamel from wire at ends
